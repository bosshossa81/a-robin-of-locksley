In the Heart of a Storm

written by Randal Prince
interpreted by Jacob Prince

the lightning flashes across the sky.
the thunder echoes his soft goodbye.
pouring rain begins to fall.
what wonderous force, God gave to all.

open your heart and mind,
experience such a find.
yourself, lost, entranced
with each droplet enhanced.

let the rains come in,
know where your mind has been.
release the feelings, from your deep,
gentle rhythms of the seep.

immerse the cleanse, embrace the inner flood.
patience to understand the wiseman’s blood.
avail yourself. break upon this blessed peace,
for even the mightiest storms one day cease.

yet fear not this parting glass.
again, tears of rain will come to pass
