Side Out

Hard and reckless against the dark
I press onward through the blinding hum.
I glance for the signal,
looking through motes of light
darting like lasers on the ethereal grid.
Closed fist.

Ball bounces once. Boom. Ba-Boom.
The concussive force radiates outward
curving around, bubbles out the roar of the crowd.
Ball bounces twice. Boom. Ba-Boom.
Brain still swirls from contact with the scorer’s table.
Swirl. Swirl.

I march forward. Left. Right-left.
Launching, the ball hovers in a frozen contemplation
disturbed as my palm strikes true.
The balloon floats like a knuckleball,
cutting to five, missing the target.
Libero three-balls.

I lurch forward into right-back. Sets goes out.
I stumble backwards, straddling the line.
My right-side’s maroon jersey is now flashing red and white.
His figure glides up, soaring through the stardust.
What wonderful hair, like strands of licorice.
He's sealing the pin.

My middle bit on the slide.
Now he’s late to close.
Their outside pounces with exhilaration
striking to exploit the shrinking hole.
The middle stretches, pressing his hand out in hope.
Ball ricochets.

My feet become paperweights.
Trudging forth. Left. Right.
The ball gently descends in mockery.
Diving, I fall to prevent the tool.
Sprawling, I reach in desperation.
They side-out.

As do I.
